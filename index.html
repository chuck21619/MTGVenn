MTG Deck Tag Analyzer

Deck List

Paste a deck export from Archidekt, Moxfield, TappedOut, or any plain text deck list.

Tags to Show

Choose which tags the charts below use. The number is how many cards in the deck have that tag.

Tag Counts

How many cards have each selected tag. Click a bar to list its cards below.

Tag Overlaps

Each square shows how many cards have both tags. Click a square to list its cards below. Showing up to the 40 most common selected tags.

UpSet Plot

Each column is one exact combination of tags, and its bar shows how many cards have only that combination. Click a column to list its cards below.

Force Graph

Each dot is a card, and lines join cards that share tags. Bigger dots have more connections. Click a dot to highlight its connections and list them below.

Card Grid

Rows are cards and columns are tags. Rows with few squares may not fit the deck.
