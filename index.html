MTG Deck Tag Analyzer

Deck List

Paste a deck list exported from Archidekt, Moxfield, TappedOut or any plain text list, one card per line.

Quantities like "1" or "1x", set codes, collector numbers, foil marks and category headers are ignored, so most exports work as they are. Double-faced and split cards need their full name, such as "Fire // Ice". Any card that can't be found is listed after you click Analyze.

Tags to Show

Tags come from Scryfall's community oracle tags, which describe what each card does. A card also gets every broader tag above its own, so a mana rock counts as ramp too. Tags about printing cycles are left out.

The number beside each tag is how many cards in your deck have it. Your choices here decide what the charts below show.

Each Analyze starts with removal, card advantage and ramp, plus up to four tags that are much more common in your deck than across all of Magic, skipping tags that cover nearly the same cards. The short list shows the 15 most common tags plus anything selected. Use search or Show all to find the rest.

Tag Counts

How many cards in your deck have each selected tag, from most to fewest.

Click a bar to list its cards below the chart.

Tag Overlaps

A grid of every pair of selected tags. Each square shows how many cards have both tags, and brighter squares mean more cards.

Click a square to list those cards below. Only the 40 most common selected tags are shown, to keep the grid readable.

UpSet Plot

Each column is one exact combination of selected tags. Its bar counts the cards that have exactly those selected tags and none of the others, so every card is counted in only one column.

The dots under each bar show which tags make up that combination. Up to 20 of the largest combinations are shown. Click a column to list its cards below.

Force Graph

Each dot is a card with at least one selected tag. Lines join cards that share at least as many selected tags as the slider is set to, and bigger dots have more connections. Cards that share a lot are pulled together, so groups of dots show themes in your deck.

Click a dot to highlight its connections and list them below, along with the tags each connection shares. Hover a dot to see the card.

Card Grid

Every card in your deck as a row, with a column for each selected tag. A filled square means the card has that tag.

Rows with few or no squares are cards that don't share the themes you selected, which can make them worth a closer look.

Connections in Your Deck

Each card's score is how strongly it ties into the rest of your deck. For every other card in the deck, the card earns points for the rarest tag the two share. Tags that are much more common in your deck than across all of Magic are worth more, and only tags on at least three of your cards count. Each other card counts once, so similar tags don't double up.

Scores use all of a card's tags, not just the selected ones, and the top card is set to 100. Basic lands are left out.

The colored parts of each bar show how much of the score comes from each of your deck's main themes. Similar tags share a color, and tags found on most of the deck count as other tags. Hover a part of a bar to see its tag.

A low score means a card shares few themes with the rest of the deck. It doesn't mean the card is bad: mana rocks, lands and strong staples often score low.

Connections Outside Your Deck

Cards that aren't in your deck, scored the same way against your deck's cards. The score is then scaled by how much of the card is about your themes, so focused cards rank above cards that only touch them.

Only Commander legal cards within your deck's colors are included, based on the combined color identity of every card in the list. Lands are left out. The top 100 are shown, with the best match set to 100.

These come from tags alone, so a card can fit your themes without being a strong card. The bar colors match the section above.
